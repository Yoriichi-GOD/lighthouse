## Shared direction

- Keep the three images visually related: painterly, atmospheric, soft-edged, and richly layered.
- Use deep blue as the darkest color; avoid black backgrounds.
- Keep focal detail and bright highlights away from likely text and panel areas.
- Export as high-quality PNG or WebP. Preserve the requested aspect ratios when the image tool allows it.
